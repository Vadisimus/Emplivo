Spa therapist CV: a practical checklist

Make your experience, practical skills and availability easy for an employer to understand.

Published and reviewed: 2026-11-12

Contact details and specialism

Include current contact details, specialism, availability and preferred country. Avoid document numbers or unnecessary sensitive information in an initial CV.

Experience and training

For each position, describe responsibilities, treatments and dates. State the training provider, qualification and dates accurately, and explain your actual language ability.

Before you submit

Check contact details and readability. The Emplivo form accepts PDF or DOCX files up to 5 MB. List any questions about the offer separately.
