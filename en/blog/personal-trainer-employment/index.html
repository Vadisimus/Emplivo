Personal trainer: employment or self-employment?

The same session price does not mean the same income or working conditions.

Published and reviewed: 2026-11-12

Understand the arrangement

Ask who contracts with clients, sets prices and schedules, and handles cancellations. The title personal trainer does not determine employment status.

Compare the costs

Consider rent, insurance, training, marketing and unpaid time. Client revenue should not be treated as equivalent to an employee salary.

Check local requirements

Tax and employment-status rules vary by country. Check official sources before signing and seek local professional advice where needed.
