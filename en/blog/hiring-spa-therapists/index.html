How to prepare a spa therapist hiring brief

A clear brief helps compare candidates by skills and expectations rather than job title alone.

Published and reviewed: 2026-11-12

Describe the working day

Specify treatments, equipment, session length, room preparation and client interaction. A generic request for an all-rounder is not a substitute for describing the work.

Separate the requirements

Distinguish essential qualifications from skills that can be taught. Check destination-country practice requirements using official sources and agree how credentials will be verified.

Prepare the conditions

Describe base pay, commission, schedule, breaks and start date. Agree interview questions so candidates receive consistent information.
