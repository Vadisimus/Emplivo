How forms work

Files and applications

Operator: Emplivo LLC, registration 46615773. The Emplivo team has access to submitted applications and CVs. For access, correction, deletion, retention or employer-sharing enquiries, contact hr@emplivo.com.ua or +380 (67) 777 80 60. You can withdraw consent through these contacts.

Telegram

If you choose the Telegram application, the bot requests consent, your name, contact, preferred role, city and experience. Only after confirmation does it send these details to the manager’s private chat. The public jobs group does not receive applications. Telegram also processes messages under its own privacy policy. Use /cancel to discard a draft; drafts cannot resume after 1 hour of inactivity. Encrypted server records are deleted after 24 hours of inactivity at the next hourly cleanup. The manager must delete received applications within 90 days. To request deletion of submitted information or withdraw consent, contact hr@emplivo.com.ua. The bot does not accept CV files.

Online forms

When a form uses Cloudflare, accepted applications and briefs are delivered to a private recruitment chat in Telegram. Rate limits and Turnstile protect the forms from spam. The reference and delivery status prevent duplicates. Daily cleanup removes records older than seven days.
